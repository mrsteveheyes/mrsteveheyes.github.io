---
title: "Day two: on small commitments"
description: "100 words is not the hard bit. The hard bit is turning up again tomorrow."
date: 2026-12-02
---

The thing I keep underestimating about daily practice is that the daily bit matters more than the practice bit. 100 words a day for a month is 3,000 words. One 3,000-word post, written on a weekend when I feel inspired, is also 3,000 words. On paper the same. In practice, not close.

The daily version makes writing a thing I do, not a thing I'm doing. By day five the resistance is gone. By day ten the ideas are queueing. By day fifteen I can't remember what it felt like to not write.

Today was 100 words on the train.
