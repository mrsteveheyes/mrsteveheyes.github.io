I have a love/hate relationship with my website. I really like 'my space' online. It has always been nice. In fact, my first website was my own space so I could 'adopt' a Pokémon (turns out it was just an HTML embed and not as cool as I hoped).

That said, I've always struggled with the 'why' of my own space. Apart from self-promotion of some form, I don't really have a good reason. I am very 'purpose-driven' and struggle to have motivation for things I don't get the 'why' of.

The main reason I am creating this section is because I want to get better at writing. I am dyslexic and find written comms something I constantly need to improve at. So, if I have a posts section, I have somewhere to put my practices.

I don't know. I am second-guessing myself. So I decided to Just Ship It <sup>TM</sup>. I can always remove it later.

It is my space.
