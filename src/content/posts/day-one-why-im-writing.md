---
title: "Day one: why I'm writing"
description: "Kicking off an Advent of Writing — 100 words a day, every day, in public."
date: 2026-12-01
---

I've been meaning to write more publicly for years. The excuse is always the same — not enough time, not enough to say, not polished enough. So this December I'm doing an Advent of Writing: at least 100 words a day, published here, for every day of Advent.

The rules are simple. Write every day. 100 words is the floor, not the ceiling. Publish before midnight. No backfilling if I miss. If I'm busy, 100 words is still 100 words — shorter is honest, not failure.

I'm not expecting any of it to be very good. I'm expecting it to exist.
