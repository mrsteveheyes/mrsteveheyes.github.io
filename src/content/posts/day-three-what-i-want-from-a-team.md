---
title: "Day three: what I want from a team"
description: "Three things I keep coming back to when a team is going well, and three when it isn't."
date: 2026-12-03
---

Three things I notice when a team is going well. People disagree in the open, cheerfully, about the work. Decisions happen at the lowest level where the information is. The team can tell you what it's trying to do this week without checking a document.

Three things when a team is going badly. The retro is the only place anyone says what they actually think. Decisions bounce back up because nobody feels safe making them. The team can tell you what it's *working on* but not what it's *trying to do*.

Neither list is a process. Both are a mood.
