title: "Day five: tools I reach for"
description: "The handful of tools I actually use most days, and why they stay."
date: 2026-12-05
---

I try a lot of tools. Most of them don't stick. The ones that do have something in common: they get out of the way.

Obsidian for notes, because the file is just a file. iA Writer for anything I want to actually finish, because there's nothing to fiddle with. Raycast for everything I'd otherwise forget. A paper notebook on the desk for whatever my hands are doing while I think.

The tools I've abandoned, almost without exception, were the ones that wanted to help me organise *before* I knew what I was doing. Organising is the last step, not the first.
