---
title: "Day four: the expensive silence"
description: "The costliest moments in a team are the ones where nobody says the thing."
date: 2026-12-04
---

The costliest moments in a team are the ones where nobody says the thing. Everyone's thinking it. Nobody says it. Two weeks later the thing has happened, and in the retro somebody finally says it, and everyone else nods, and it's too late.

The reason is almost never that people don't know. It's that the cost of saying it is higher than the cost of not saying it, right in that moment. The fix isn't courage. The fix is lowering the cost of the first person saying the awkward thing.

I'm still working out how to do that reliably.
